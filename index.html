Hello, I'm Hazem.

I am an AI Software Engineer and Data Scientist with a Master's degree in Telecommunications from the Institut national de la recherche scientifique (INRS) and an engineering background in ICT and telecommunications.

My experience spans applied AI development and academic research. At B-CITI Solutions, I worked on AI-powered municipal services and agent-based applications. I am particularly interested in building reliable LLM-powered applications through better retrieval, evaluation, orchestration, and multilingual language technologies.

Outside my professional work, I built and open-sourced a Municipal AI Agent demo: a bilingual FAQ assistant with LangGraph, Qdrant and Azure OpenAI, tested and evaluated on synthetic data.
